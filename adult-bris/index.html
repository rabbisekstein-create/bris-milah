Adult Bris Milah

For men who did not have the opportunity as newborns. Performed with anesthesia, with real attention to comfort, and in complete confidence.

If you are reading this page, you are not unusual.

Men come to this at every age and from every background — some who grew up without the opportunity, some who came to observance later in life, some completing a conversion, some who simply were never able to have it done. Many have carried the question quietly for years, unsure who they could even ask.

You can ask me. I am trained specifically for adult Bris Milah, and I have carried out this work together with the well-known Mohel Rabbi Aron Weiss. The first conversation costs nothing, commits you to nothing, and stays entirely between us.

How it works

Four stages, no surprises

1

A private conversation

We speak by phone, at whatever length you need. You tell me your situation; I tell you honestly what is involved. Nothing is arranged until you are ready.

2

Preparation

I send you two short forms: one about your background and what brought you to this, and one about your medical history, which is reviewed by a medical professional before anything is scheduled. The doctor reads the medical form; nobody reads the other but me. What to have ready.

3

The procedure

Performed under anesthesia, with proper pain management throughout. The religious requirements are carried out fully and correctly, together with the blessings.

4

Recovery

You leave with clear written instructions and my direct number. I follow up through the healing period, and I am available for any question that comes up.

Comfort and discretion

Two things matter more than anything else here, and they are the two things men ask about first.

Comfort. Adult procedures are done with anesthesia — you are not expected to endure this. Pain management is planned in advance, not improvised, and the recovery is managed carefully. I will walk you through exactly what to expect, including honestly what the first few days feel like.

Discretion. This is a private matter and I treat it that way. Your conversation with me is confidential. Nothing is discussed with anyone, and nothing about your situation goes further than the two of us.

Common questions

How long is recovery?
Most men are back to desk work within a few days, with a longer period before strenuous activity. I will give you a realistic picture for your situation.

Do I need to travel?
It depends where you are. I travel regularly, and much of my adult work has been done away from home. We will find something that works.

Is this connected to conversion?
For some men it is, and I work with the beis din involved. For others it has nothing to do with conversion at all. Either way, you are welcome.

One conversation.

You do not have to decide anything to have it. Ask your questions, and take as long as you need afterwards. If a message is easier than a call, write to me on WhatsApp.
