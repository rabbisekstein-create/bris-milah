Consent

1. The procedure. I am asking Rabbi Shlome Ekstein to perform the procedure marked above, as we discussed and agreed. It will take place at the location we agreed on, during the day, under local anesthesia. Rabbi Ekstein has explained the main steps, the risks, and the aftercare in English. If English is not my first language, I was able to ask for a translation and ask questions until I understood, before signing.

2. Who is performing it. I understand that Rabbi Ekstein is a certified mohel with over 15 years of experience. He is not a medical doctor. Another mohel or an assistant may be present to help him.

3. Possible risks. I understand that, as with any procedure, there are risks, including: pain, swelling, or bruising during healing; bleeding, which may need extra care; infection; a reaction to the local anesthetic; a cosmetic result different from what I expected; and in rare cases, the need for further treatment or a doctor's care.

4. Other options. I understand I may choose not to have the procedure, or to have it done by a physician instead.

5. My health information. The health information I gave Rabbi Ekstein, on my intake form or in person, is true and complete to the best of my knowledge, and I have told him about any change in my health since.

6. Aftercare. I have received written aftercare instructions and agree to follow them. I will call Rabbi Ekstein with any question, and get emergency medical care right away for bleeding that does not stop with steady pressure, fever, or trouble urinating.

7. Privacy. My information is kept confidential. My forms are seen only by Rabbi Ekstein, unless I agree otherwise or the law requires it. Anyone helping him on the day also keeps everything confidential.

8. My right to change my mind. I may withdraw this consent at any time before the procedure begins. Rabbi Ekstein went over this form with me and answered my questions.
