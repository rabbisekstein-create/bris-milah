Questions men ask

Can I keep it completely private?

Yes. Many men prefer that nobody knows, not their family, not their community, not their work. That is entirely your choice, and I respect it fully.

Our conversations stay between us

You can reach me by phone or WhatsApp, at whatever time suits you. What you tell me stays between us. I do not share your name or your story with anyone.

You do not need to give me your full story at the start, either. Ask your questions first, and decide later.

A private room, only the two of us

The brit is done in a private room, with only me and my assistant. It can be in my office or at a place we arrange together.

Nobody else needs to be there. If you would like a rabbi or a friend present, or a small celebration afterwards, we can do that too. Some men choose to keep it private and celebrate later.

Planned around your life

We choose a date that fits your work and family, so you can rest for a few days without needing to explain. I travel, so you do not have to come to New York if you would rather not.

And cost should never be the reason. Nobody is turned away over money; when needed, a sponsor covers it, and that is also kept private.

Short answers

Will anyone have to know?

No. Our conversations are confidential, and if you prefer to keep it private, nobody else needs to know.

Who is in the room?

Only me and my assistant, unless you choose to invite someone.

Can you come to me?

Yes. I travel across the United States and internationally, so we can plan it where it is easiest for you.
