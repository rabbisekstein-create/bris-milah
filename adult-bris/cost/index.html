Questions men ask

What does an adult brit milah cost?

A clear answer, up front: the usual fee is $1,800–$2,000. Here is what that includes, and what to do if cost is a problem.

The usual fee

An adult brit milah usually costs $1,800–$2,000. I tell you where your situation falls on the first call, before anything is arranged, so you are never guessing.

If cost is a real obstacle, say so. Nobody is turned away over money. We can talk about it privately, and where it is right, I can arrange a sponsor.

What the fee covers

  • Private conversations by phone or WhatsApp, as many as you need, with me directly
  • Help preparing, including going through your medical history with you before anything is scheduled
  • The brit itself, under local anesthesia, with a pain plan agreed in advance
  • Sterile, single-use instruments
  • Clear written aftercare instructions
  • My own cell number, and follow-up through the whole healing period

Before you decide

Price matters, but so does who you are trusting. Ask any mohel how many adult procedures he has done, who you will speak with when you have a question at night, and what happens if you are worried on day three.

With me, you speak to me — from the first call to full recovery. Read more about adult brit milah or what recovery is like.
