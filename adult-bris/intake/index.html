Adult Bris Milah — Confidential Intake

Thank you for reaching out. Choosing a bris as an adult takes real courage and commitment, and it is a privilege to help you enter the covenant of Avraham Avinu.

What to expect. Bris milah is performed here only as a Jewish religious rite, for Jewish men and those converting to Judaism. This form takes about 10 minutes. It has four short parts: about you, your bris, your medical history, and scheduling. Everything you share is kept private and is seen only by Rabbi Ekstein and, where needed for your safety, a reviewing physician.

After you submit, Rabbi Ekstein will personally call you to talk things through and answer every question — nothing is scheduled until you've spoken. This form is not a commitment and not a consent to the procedure.
