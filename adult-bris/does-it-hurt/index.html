Questions men ask

Does an adult brit hurt?

It is the question almost every man asks first, even if he asks it last. Here is the honest answer, step by step.

Before it starts

An adult brit milah is done under local anesthesia. The area is numbed with a few small injections, each felt as a quick pinch for a few seconds. That is usually the only part that really stings.

We wait until the area is fully numb before anything else begins.

During the brit

Once the anesthesia has taken effect, you should not feel pain. You may be aware of touch or pressure, but not of cutting. You are awake the whole time, and you can talk to me throughout.

It is done in a private room, with only me and my assistant. You can take your time, ask questions, and we go at your pace.

The days after

When the anesthesia wears off, it is normal to feel sore for some days, like any healing wound. Pain management is planned in advance, so you know what to take and when before you leave.

I give you my cell number, and you can call me with any question, however small. More detail is on the recovery page.

The fear is usually bigger than the brit

Many men carry this fear for years before they call. The waiting and wondering are often the hardest part. A conversation costs nothing and commits you to nothing, and I will answer every question honestly.

Short answers

Am I awake during an adult brit?

Yes. It is done under local anesthesia. You are awake and can talk the whole time, but the area is numb.

What does the anesthesia feel like?

A few quick pinches from the injections, each lasting a few seconds. After that, the area is numb.

How sore will I be afterwards?

It is normal to be sore for some days as it heals. Pain management is planned before the brit, and you can reach me any time with questions.

How long until I can go back to work?

It depends on your work. We plan the date around your schedule. See the recovery page for details.
