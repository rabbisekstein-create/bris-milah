Exploring your Jewish roots

Perhaps you've learned that your mother, father or grandmother was Jewish, or you're finding your way back after years away. You're welcome here, whatever your story.

Before a brit milah or hatafas dam bris, there's a question that comes first: your Jewish status. That depends on your family, so this short form asks about your parents. Here's how it works:

  1. A few questionsAbout your parents and whether you've spoken with a rabbi. About three minutes. No documents and no medical questions.
  2. A private conversationRabbi Ekstein calls or texts you back and answers your questions.
  3. A rabbi's guidanceQuestions of Jewish status are decided by a rabbi or beis din, not by this form. Where that's needed, we help arrange it, and work with your own rabbi if you have one.
  4. Only then, the britIf and when you're ready, the usual adult steps follow, and not before.

Your privacy. Your answers are read only by Rabbi Ekstein and used only to call you back and guide your next step. Nothing is tracked on this form. How your information is handled
