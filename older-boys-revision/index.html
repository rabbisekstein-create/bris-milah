For parents

Do you do revisions for older boys?

Yes. Many parents worry, years later, that their son’s circumcision wasn’t done completely.

Start with a private conversation

Tell me what you’ve noticed and how the circumcision was done, for example in a hospital. We talk it through privately, parent and rabbi.

Please don’t send photos. It’s hard to tell from a picture, and for a child I only look in person.

A parent is always present

Any check is done in person, with a parent present. Every step is explained beforehand, and nothing is decided until your questions are answered.

Cost

Cost is discussed privately. I don’t want money to be the reason a boy doesn’t get the help he needs.
