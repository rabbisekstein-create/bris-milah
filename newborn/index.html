Mazel Tov!

Your checklist for the bris

At the hospital

  • Write down the exact time of birthIf he was born close to sunset, your rav will need it to set the date of the bris.
  • Ask for his bilirubin (jaundice) resultsGet the numbers before discharge. Your mohel may ask to see them.
  • Keep the pediatrician’s notesBirth weight and anything the doctors mentioned.

Planning the bris

  • Book your mohel earlyCall as soon as you can, so the date and time are set.
  • Confirm the date with your ravEspecially if the birth was close to sunset or on Shabbos.
  • Choose the place and arrange a minyanShul, hall, or home. Let your mohel know where it will be.
  • Decide on the honorsSandek, kvatter, and the rest. Use the printable list below.
  • Keep your mohel updatedTell him about any change in the baby’s health, color, or feeding before the bris.

The day of the bris

  • Have the supplies your mohel asks forHe’ll tell you exactly what to prepare.
  • Bring the honors listSo everyone knows when they’re called.
  • Follow your mohel’s aftercare instructionsAnd call him with any question, day or night.
