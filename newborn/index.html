Before you leave the hospital

Your mohel will ask about these. Get the answers before you go home, and ask for a copy of the discharge papers.

  • Birth date and exact timeIt can matter for the bris date. If he was born close to sunset, your rav will need it.
  • Born on time or early?How many weeks?
  • WeightAt birth and at discharge.
  • Jaundice (bilirubin)The latest number, and the date and time it was checked. Was any treatment given (bili lights)?
  • FeverAny? When, and how high?
  • BreathingDid he need any help breathing (oxygen, NICU)? For how long?
  • Antibiotics or other medicinesWhich, when started, and when finished?
  • Anything else the doctors watched or testedWhat, and the result.

Then send the details to your mohel as soon as you can, so the bris can be planned on time.

Planning the bris

  • Book your mohel earlyCall as soon as you can, so the date and time are set.
  • Confirm the date with your ravEspecially if the birth was close to sunset or on Shabbos.
  • Choose the place and arrange a minyanShul, hall, or home. Let your mohel know where it will be.
  • Decide on the honorsSandek, kvatter, and the rest. Use the printable list below.
  • Keep your mohel updatedTell him about any change in the baby’s health, color, or feeding before the bris.

The day of the bris

  • Have the supplies your mohel asks forHe’ll tell you exactly what to prepare.
  • Bring the honors listSo everyone knows when they’re called.
  • Follow your mohel’s aftercare instructionsAnd call him with any question, day or night.
