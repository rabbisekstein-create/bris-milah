Brit milah for conversion

If you are converting, a brit milah or a hatafas dam bris is part of the process. I work directly with your beis din so it is done correctly and on time.

Two situations

If you are not circumcised, a full brit milah is part of your conversion. For an adult it is done under local anesthesia, with a pain plan agreed in advance, in a private setting.

If you are already circumcised — for example, in hospital as a baby — you usually need a hatafas dam bris: a symbolic drop of blood that brings the circumcision under the covenant. It is a brief procedure.

Either way, your beis din decides what is required for you. I follow their instructions exactly.

Short answers

Do I need a full brit milah, or hatafas dam bris?

It depends on whether you are already circumcised. If you are not, a full brit milah is needed. If you are, it is usually a hatafas dam bris. Your beis din decides what is required in your case, and I follow their instructions.

Do you work with my beis din?

Yes. I coordinate with them directly and I am used to the paperwork and timing that go with a conversion.

Does it hurt?

An adult brit milah is done under local anesthesia, with a pain plan agreed in advance. A hatafas dam bris is a brief procedure.

Can you come to where I live?

Yes. I travel across the United States and internationally, including Latin America.

Is it private?

Yes. Our conversations are confidential, and your forms are read only by me.
