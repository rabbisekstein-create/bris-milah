Travel and destination

Where there is no Mohel nearby, I come to you. Across the United States, and internationally.

No family should have to go without.

Not every community has a Mohel. Some have one who is unavailable on the day it must happen, which, with an eighth day, is not a date anyone can move. Families in that position have often spent a week making frantic phone calls.

I have travelled for this work for years. Together with my Rabbi, Rabbi Yosef Heilbrun, I have performed approximately 40 procedures across Latin America, and I serve families throughout the United States. If you are somewhere without a Mohel, call me and we will work out whether I can get to you.

Where I have served

Communities across the United States, and internationally — with particular experience across Latin America.

What I bring

Everything required, including all sterile single-use instruments. You do not need to source anything locally.

Who I work with

Your local Rav, your community, and where relevant your local physician — so that everything is correct both halachically and practically.

Languages

I speak English and Yiddish, and I understand Hebrew. In Latin America I have worked alongside local rabbanim and my Rabbi, Rabbi Yosef Heilbrun, so families there have always been able to arrange everything in their own language.

Arranging it

How a destination Bris comes together

1

Call as early as you can

With travel involved, timing is everything. Call during the pregnancy if you possibly can — even a provisional conversation helps enormously.

2

We confirm the day

Once the baby is born, we fix the eighth day precisely and I arrange travel around it. I plan to arrive with time in hand, never at the last moment.

3

Local coordination

I speak with your Rav or community if you would like, and we settle the venue, the honors and anything needed on the ground.

4

The day, and afterwards

The Bris takes place exactly as it would anywhere else, and I remain reachable for the healing period after I have travelled home.

Not sure whether I can reach you?

Ask anyway. The answer is more often yes than people expect, and it costs nothing to find out.
